About The Game

True Cookie Reincarnation is a dungeon crawler turned mystery game inspired by traditional first person apocalyptic exploration games from the 1990s.

Dive into the mysteries of your school, recently overrun by cookie demons.

Come to understand the root of it all and face the monsters alongside the people you meet.

Some may help, and some may hinder your ability on your journey.

Can you save your school in time before your world is enveloped by the cookiedimensional plane and overrun by the cookie demons?
